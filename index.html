    The Emerg-ant Hackathon

    Ants are the standing argument that intelligence does not need a head office. A colony has no manager and no blueprint, and it still finds the shortest route to your kitchen before you have finished washing up.

    So: build something ant-shaped. Pheromone fields, foraging, stigmergy, brood sorting, bridges made out of bodies, or a single ant that refuses on principle to cooperate.

    Start from nants ↗ or bring your own colony

    nants is a small browser ant sandbox — a floor, some ants, a pheromone field — that you can fork and have running in about a minute. Starting from scratch is equally welcome. The only rule is that ants are involved somewhere.
