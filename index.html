    🐜 The Emerg-ant Hackathon

    Ants are the standing argument that intelligence does not need a head office. A colony has no manager and no blueprint, and it still finds the shortest route to your kitchen before you have finished washing up.

    So: build something ant like.

    • a turn rule that is learned, not written down
    • two crowds that disagree which way is up
    • stigmergy
    • foraging
    • brood sorting
    • bridges made out of bodies
    Start from NAnts ↗ or bring your own colony

    NAnts — neural ants: Langton’s ant made trainable, inspired by the NCA architecture. Fork it, or start from scratch.
